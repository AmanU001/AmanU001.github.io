Director's Commentary · about me

Slow but persistent. Growing. Human.

Transport decides who gets to school, to work, to a hospital — and who gets left behind. That's why I do this. I've learned it inside institutions that each taught me a piece of it: the World Bank's mission to end poverty on a livable planet, the UN's promise to leave no one behind — safe, affordable, sustainable transport for all — and GIZ's way of working side by side with local partners, so the systems we build belong to the people who run them long after we leave.

I'm rarely the fastest person in the room. I'm usually the one still there — measuring, asking, fixing.
